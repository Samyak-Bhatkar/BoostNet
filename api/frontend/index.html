📱 How to Use BoostNet on Mobile

BoostNet bonds multiple phones (Airtel + Jio + Vi) into one unified broadband pipe for your college laptop, bypassing library congestion without cables or root access.

How It Works:

  1. Step 1 — Connect to Local Wi-Fi: Keep your laptop and phones on the same Wi-Fi (e.g. laptop hotspot or dorm router).
  2. Step 2 — Scan Pairing QR: Click the QR Code button in the header from your laptop, and scan the QR code using your phone's camera.
  3. Step 3 — Dual-Transport Binding: The BoostNet mobile worker keeps local Wi-Fi active for laptop talk while routing download chunks over cellular (4G/5G).
  4. Step 4 — Donor Trust Limits: Set custom limits directly from the dashboard: e.g. maximum 250 MB data cap and pause if phone battery drops below 25%.
💡 Live Cloud Showcase: When viewing on Vercel, click "⚡ Simulate 3 Phones" or "⚡ Turbo Download" to watch the multi-carrier aggregation demo right in your browser!

About BoostNet Architecture

BoostNet aggregates cellular data connections across nearby mobile devices using local Wi-Fi mesh networking and dynamic micro-chunk multiplexing, delivering multi-carrier throughput to college laptops without requiring paid cloud servers or USB cables.
